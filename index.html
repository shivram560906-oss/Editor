Write

Write your story

Three blank lines start a new slide. Use ## for a title and # for a subtitle.

Writing rules (tap to open)
  1. First line is always the main heading.
  2. ## text makes a title.
  3. # text makes a subtitle. Several ## lines in a row make a multi-line title.
  4. Anything without ## or # is plain body text.
  5. Press Enter once: the next text goes on a new line in the video.
  6. Press Enter three times: a new slide.
  7. A full stop never creates a slide or a break.
  8. Lists: - text bullet, 1. text number. Indent with 2 spaces.
