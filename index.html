Writing rules (click to open or close)
  1. First line is always the main heading.
  2. ## text makes a title.
  3. # text makes a subtitle. Several ## lines (or # lines) in a row make a multi-line title (or subtitle).
  4. Anything without ## or # is plain body text. No line is ever guessed to be a heading.
  5. Press Enter once or twice: nothing special. The text stays in the same block.
  6. Press Enter three times: a new slide. Nothing else starts a new slide.
  7. A full stop . or Nepali full stop । never creates a slide or a break.
  8. Lists: - text bullet, 1. text number, 2 spaces in front = sub-level (up to 4 levels). Or use the buttons above.
